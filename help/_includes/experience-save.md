# Steps to save an experience

<!-- used in multiple procedures -->

1. To save the experience, click **[!UICONTROL Save]** in the decision tree header.

   In the AI-driven editor (Beta), wait for the assistant to finish processing before saving.

1. If a warning indicates that scheduling is incomplete, click **[!UICONTROL OK]**, correct the schedules so that bundles cover all dates in the ad schedule, and then click **[!UICONTROL Save]** again.

1. In the save confirmation dialog, do one of the following:

   * If every final node has assigned creative bundles or uses default creatives, click **[!UICONTROL OK]** in the **[!UICONTROL Save & Publish]** dialog to save and publish the tree.

   * If any final nodes don't have creatives, choose one of the following:

      * Click **[!UICONTROL Save as Draft]** to save without publishing. You can't generate an ad tag for a [draft](/help/creative/experiences/experience-about.md#experience-statuses) experience.

      * Click **[!UICONTROL Assign Default Creatives]** to use the experience's default creatives for unassigned nodes. Review the updated tree, and then click **[!UICONTROL Save]** and **[!UICONTROL OK]**.

      * Click **[!UICONTROL Continue Edit]** to return to the tree without saving.
