## [!UICONTROL Adgroup Details]

**[!UICONTROL ad Name]:** An ad name that's unique within the campaign. The maximum length is 15 double-byte characters.

**[!UICONTROL Status]:** The display status of the ad: *Active* or *Paused*. The default for new ads is *Active*.
