# [!DNL ChatGPT Ads] ad settings

Ads in [!DNL ChatGPT] campaigns are shown alongside AI chats in [!DNL ChatGPT].

<!-- Create, edit, delete. Possibly not all of the other procedures available to ads on the other SEs. -->

## [!UICONTROL Basic Settings] tab

*New campaigns only*

**[!UICONTROL Network]:** The ad network.

**[!UICONTROL Account]:** The ad network account.

**[!UICONTROL Campaign]:** The campaign.

**[!UICONTROL Ad Group]:** The ad group.

## [!UICONTROL Ad Details] tab

**[!UICONTROL Ad Name]:** An ad name that's unique within the ad group.

**[!UICONTROL Status]:** The display status of the ad: *Active* or *Paused*. The default for new ads is *Active*.

**[!UICONTROL Title]:** The ad title. The maximum length is 50 characters.

**[!UICONTROL Description]:** The body of the ad. The maximum length is 100 characters.

**[!UICONTROL Link]:** The landing page URL.

**[!UICONTROL Image]:** A JPG or PNG file to include in the ad. The maximum file size is 20 MB. For the best image quality, use a square image of 256 x 256 px or higher.

To upload an image, either drag and drop a file into the box, or click the box and select a file from your device or network.
