# Generate reusable audiences using AI Assistant

*Beta feature*

*Support for English only*

Use the AI Assistant's audience builder agent to generate [reusable audiences](/help/dsp/audiences/audience-about.md). Reusable audiences can include any first-party and third-party segments that are available to you, and even other saved audiences.

>[!IMPORTANT]
>
>AI-generated responses may be inaccurate or misleading. Always verify responses and sources before using them for decisions that affect cost or effort.

## Example queries

* High-income homeowners (HHI $75k+) who are environmentally conscious and are actively shop for renewable energy solutions and energy-efficient appliances.

* Urban professionals aged 25-45 who are currently researching wellness retreats and luxury fitness tours and have interests in boutique yoga and marathon running. The segment CPM shouldn't exceed $3 and should be targetable on mobile apps.

* Food-focused individuals in Australia who frequently shop at specialty grocers and are currently in-market for high-end cookware or premium subscription meal kits.

## Submit a query

See "[Create a reusable audience](/help/dsp/audiences/reusable-audience-create.md)."

## Start a new conversation {#conversation-new}

Each conversation, or query thread, is saved separately so you can distinguish between your previous conversations.

1. At the top of the [chatbox](#ai-assistant-open), click ![Start a new conversation](/help/dsp/assets/new-selection.png "Start a new conversation").

1. [Submit a query](#query-submit).

## Expand and minimize the chatbox

1. At the top of the [chatbox](#ai-assistant-open), click ![Enter fullscreen](/help/dsp/assets/fullscreen.png "Enter fullscreen") to enter fullscreen mode.

1. To return the chatbox to panel mode, click **[!UICONTROL < Back>]**.
