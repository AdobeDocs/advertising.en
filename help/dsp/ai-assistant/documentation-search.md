# Search for product documentation using AI Assistant

*Support for English only*

Use AI Assistant for DSP to search for conceptual and how-to content across the [Advertising DSP Guide](/help/dsp/home.md) and (advertisers with Advertising Creative) the [Advertising Creative Guide](/help/creative/home.md) using a chat interface. Answers are based only on what's documented for these products in [Experience League](https://experienceleague.adobe.com/en/docs/advertising).

You can ask about campaign management, optimization, audience management, deals, reports, and other product features. Responses include citations as well as additional queries and follow-up questions to help you refine your query and find more information.

![Example of a query and response](/help/dsp/assets/agent-chat-response.png "Example of a query and response")

>[!IMPORTANT]
>
>AI-generated responses may be inaccurate or misleading. Always verify responses and sources before using them for decisions that affect cost or effort.

## Example queries

* How do I attach an ad to a placement?

* What targeting options are available in Adobe DSP?

* What are the consequences of using the different pacing options in placement settings?

* Which reports include household-level data?

* How do I create an ad tag for a [!DNL Creative] experience?

## Submit a query

You can ask multiple questions in one message, but only one message at a time. Wait for a response before sending another.

1. In the upper right of any page, click ![Agentic Chat](/help/dsp/assets/agent-chat.png "Agentic chat").

1. Build the query:

   * Enter your own query.
   
   * To see examples of queries, click **[!UICONTROL Learn]** to expand the section.
     
     1. (Optional) To copy the text of an example query into the input field:
     
        1. Click the example.
        
        1. (Optional) Edit the text to suit your needs. 

   For more information about queries, see "[Best practices for writing queries and prompts](ai-assistant-about.md#writing-queries)."

1. Click ![Submit query](/help/dsp/assets/submit-prompt.png "Submit query") or press the **Enter** key on your keyboard.

   The response includes inline citations and a **[!UICONTROL Documentation Sources]** list at the bottom. Follow-up questions and suggestions may also appear.

1. If AI Assistant asks for additional information, provide it.

1. To open a page used as a data source for the response, do either of the following:

   * Click the numbered citation.

   * Click **[!UICONTROL Documentation Sources]** to show a list of all pages cited in the response, and then click the page link.

>[!TIP]
>
>To ask about a different issue, or about a different package or placement, [start a new conversation](#conversation-new).

## Start a new conversation {#conversation-new}

Each conversation, or query thread, is saved separately so you can distinguish between your previous conversations.

1. At the top of the [chatbox](#ai-assistant-open), click ![Start a new conversation](/help/dsp/assets/new-selection.png "Start a new conversation").

1. [Submit a query](#query-submit).
